# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO ONLY

**Tender No.:** T102-2026
**Issuing entity:** Northern Region Development Authority (fictional)
**Title:** AI analytics platform for smart city operations
**Estimated budget:** SAR 9,000,000
**Contract duration:** 24 months
**Submission deadline:** 2026-12-15, 13:00 Riyadh time

## 1. Background
The Authority is building a smart city operations center and needs an AI analytics platform that combines traffic, utilities and citizen-report data into dashboards and predictive alerts.

## 2. Scope of work
1. Data platform ingesting data from existing city systems (APIs and files).
2. Predictive analytics for traffic congestion and utility faults.
3. Executive dashboards in Arabic and English.
4. Training for 40 operations staff.

## 3. Eligibility requirements (mandatory)
3.1 Valid Commercial Registration, Zakat, GOSI and Nitaqat certificates.
3.2 ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
3.3 Minimum local content score of 35%.

## 4. Technical requirements
4.1 At least one data platform or dashboard project for a government entity in the last five years. (mandatory)
4.2 Hosting inside the Kingdom. (mandatory)
4.3 Arabic-first user interface. (desirable)

## 5. Evaluation criteria
- Technical: 60%
- Financial: 40%

## 6. Submission documents
- Technical and financial proposals
- Bid bond: 2% of bid value
- Compliance matrix (see Appendix B)

## Appendix A — Integration list
Traffic management system, water utility SCADA exports (read-only CSV), citizen reporting app.

## Appendix B — Additional bidder qualifications
B.1 Bidders must hold an active **CMMI Level 3 (Development) or higher** appraisal at the time of submission. This requirement is mandatory; bids from companies without a valid CMMI Level 3 appraisal will be disqualified without technical evaluation.
B.2 The bidder must also supply and install 120 roadside IoT sensors, or name a qualified installation subcontractor in the bid.

# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO ONLY

**Tender No.:** T101-2026
**Issuing entity:** General Authority for Citizen Services (fictional)
**Title:** Design, build and operate a bilingual AI virtual assistant for citizen services
**Estimated budget:** SAR 6,500,000
**Contract duration:** 18 months (12 months build + 6 months operation)
**Submission deadline:** 2026-12-01, 13:00 Riyadh time
**Questions deadline:** 2026-11-15

## 1. Background
The Authority receives about 400,000 citizen enquiries per month through its call center and web portal. It seeks an AI virtual assistant to answer enquiries in Arabic and English, guide citizens through services, and hand over complex cases to human agents.

## 2. Scope of work
1. Conversational AI assistant on web portal, mobile app and WhatsApp.
2. Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic and Saudi dialects) and English.
3. Integration with the Authority's case-management system via REST APIs.
4. Admin console for content updates and analytics dashboard.
5. Six months of operation and support after go-live, with 99.5% availability.

## 3. Eligibility requirements (mandatory)
3.1 Valid Commercial Registration.
3.2 Valid Zakat certificate from ZATCA.
3.3 Valid GOSI certificate.
3.4 Valid Saudization (Nitaqat) certificate.
3.5 ISO/IEC 27001 certification, valid at submission.
3.6 Minimum local content score of 40% (LCGPA certified).

## 4. Technical requirements
4.1 The bidder shall have delivered at least **two (2)** conversational AI projects in the last five years, each valued above SAR 2,000,000, supported by client reference letters. (mandatory)
4.2 All data, models and logs must be hosted inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. (mandatory)
4.3 The solution must comply with NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC). (mandatory)
4.4 Personal data processing must comply with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). (mandatory)
4.5 Proposed project manager must hold PMP or equivalent. (mandatory)
4.6 Human handover with full conversation context. (mandatory)

## 5. Evaluation criteria
- Technical proposal: 70% (experience 25%, methodology 25%, team 20%)
- Financial proposal: 30%
- Minimum technical score to pass: 65%

## 6. Submission documents
- Technical proposal, financial proposal (separate envelopes)
- Bid bond: 1% of bid value, valid 90 days
- Compliance matrix responding to every requirement in sections 3 and 4

## Appendix A — Dialect coverage
The assistant must understand at minimum Najdi and Hijazi dialects. Gulf and Southern dialects are desirable (not mandatory).

# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO ONLY

**Tender No.:** T103-2026
**Issuing entity:** Central Specialist Hospital Cluster (fictional)
**Title:** AI-assisted radiology triage system
**Estimated budget:** SAR 7,200,000
**Contract duration:** 24 months
**Submission deadline:** 2026-11-25, 13:00 Riyadh time

## 1. Background
The hospital cluster wants an AI system that reads chest X-rays and CT scans, flags urgent findings, and prioritizes the radiologist worklist.

## 2. Scope of work
1. Supply an AI triage software medical device integrated with PACS and RIS.
2. Clinical validation on local data.
3. Training for radiologists and technicians.

## 3. Eligibility requirements (mandatory)
3.1 Valid Commercial Registration, Zakat, GOSI and Nitaqat certificates.
3.2 The proposed software must hold a valid **SFDA Medical Device Marketing Authorization (MDMA)**.
3.3 ISO 13485 certification for the manufacturer or its authorized representative.
3.4 At least **ten (10) years** of experience delivering healthcare IT solutions in the Kingdom.

## 4. Technical requirements
4.1 Integration with PACS using DICOM and HL7/FHIR. (mandatory)
4.2 Sensitivity of at least 95% on critical findings in clinical validation. (mandatory)
4.3 Hosting inside the Kingdom. (mandatory)

## 5. Evaluation criteria
- Technical: 70%, Financial: 30%
